Turn one photo into a wall of instant prints

Framosaic splits a single photo into a grid of tiles sized for real instant-film formats — Fujifilm Instax Mini, Square, and Wide, Polaroid 600/i-Type, Polaroid Go, or your own custom dimensions — so you can print each tile individually and reassemble them into one large mosaic on your wall. Everything runs entirely in your browser: your photo is never uploaded to a server, and no cropping, adjustment, or export step ever leaves your own device.

How it works

Upload a photo — it never leaves your browser. Pick an instant-film format and a grid size, then crop and adjust the image with a live preview that shows realistic film frames, a wall background, and tile numbers as you work. When you're ready, export a ZIP of correctly named, correctly sized tile images plus a PDF gluing template: a numbered overview and legend showing exactly where each print goes. No instant-film printer? Home-print mode prints each tile at true physical size on regular paper instead.

Formats

Every major instant-film size is supported out of the box: Instax Mini (54×86mm film, 46×62mm image area), Instax Square (72×86mm film, 62×62mm image area), Instax Wide (108×86mm film, 99×62mm image area), Polaroid 600 / i-Type (88×107mm film, 79×79mm image area), and Polaroid Go (53.9×66.6mm film, 46×47mm image area) — or enter any custom film and image-area dimensions of your own.

See it on the wall

However you crop it, your mosaic ends up looking like this — a grid of real instant-film prints, complete with their characteristic white borders, reconstructing one photo. The live editor mirrors this exact layout as you adjust your format, grid, and gaps, with a resolution check that flags any tile that would print too soft before you export.
